# Spanish Preterite vs Imperfect

Spanish has two simple past tenses with different jobs.

The **preterite** describes completed actions at a specific time. It answers
"what happened?" Example: *Ayer fui al mercado* (Yesterday I went to the
market). Regular endings: -ar verbs take -e, -aste, -o, -amos, -asteis, -aron;
-er and -ir verbs take -i, -iste, -io, -imos, -isteis, -ieron. Common irregulars
include ser/ir (fui, fuiste, fue...), tener (tuve...), and hacer (hice...).

The **imperfect** describes ongoing, habitual, or background actions. It answers
"what was happening?" Example: *Cuando era nino, jugaba futbol* (When I was a
child, I used to play soccer). Endings: -ar verbs -aba, -abas, -aba...; -er/-ir
verbs -ia, -ias, -ia... Only three verbs are irregular: ser, ir, ver.
