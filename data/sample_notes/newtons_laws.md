# Newton's Three Laws of Motion

**First law (inertia).** An object at rest stays at rest, and an object in
motion stays in motion at constant velocity, unless acted on by a net external
force. This is why a passenger lurches forward when a bus brakes.

**Second law.** The net force on an object equals its mass times its
acceleration: F = m * a. A heavier mass needs a larger force for the same
acceleration. Force is measured in newtons (kg*m/s^2).

**Third law.** For every action there is an equal and opposite reaction.
Forces always come in pairs acting on *different* bodies: the Earth pulls a
falling apple with the same force the apple pulls the Earth, but the Earth's
enormous mass means its acceleration is negligible.
