# How to Study Effectively

**Spaced practice** beats cramming. Review material a day later, then a week
later, then a month later; each review strengthens recall far more than one long
session.

**Active recall** means testing yourself rather than rereading. Close the book
and try to write down what you remember, or use flashcards. Getting it slightly
wrong and correcting yourself is more useful than passively recognizing text.

**Interleaving** means mixing problem types in one sitting instead of drilling
one kind. It feels harder but produces better transfer to exams.

**Elaboration** means explaining *why* a fact is true and connecting it to what
you already know. Teaching a concept to someone else (or to an empty room) is one
of the strongest known techniques.

Sleep and breaks are not optional: memory consolidation happens during sleep.
