# Cellular Respiration

Cellular respiration releases energy stored in glucose. It is roughly the
reverse of photosynthesis:

    C6H12O6 + 6 O2 -> 6 CO2 + 6 H2O + ATP

It has three main stages. **Glycolysis** occurs in the cytoplasm and splits one
glucose into two pyruvate molecules, netting 2 ATP and 2 NADH. **The Krebs
cycle** (citric acid cycle) occurs in the mitochondrial matrix and produces
NADH, FADH2, and a little ATP. **Oxidative phosphorylation** occurs across the
inner mitochondrial membrane, where the electron transport chain and ATP
synthase generate most of the ATP (about 32-34 per glucose). Oxygen is the final
electron acceptor.
