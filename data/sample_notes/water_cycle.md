# The Water Cycle

Water continuously moves between the ocean, atmosphere, and land.

**Evaporation** turns liquid water into vapor, driven by solar energy; most of
it comes from the oceans. **Transpiration** is the release of water vapor from
plant leaves. Together these are called **evapotranspiration**. **Condensation**
turns vapor back into tiny droplets, forming clouds when air cools. **Precip-
itation** returns water to the surface as rain, snow, sleet, or hail.
**Infiltration** soaks water into the ground, recharging aquifers, while
**runoff** flows over the surface into rivers and back to the ocean.

The cycle has no true starting point and is powered by the sun. It is a major
driver of weather and of the distribution of fresh water on Earth.
