# Causes of the French Revolution

Several long-term and short-term causes combined.

**Long-term causes.** The Ancien Regime was rigidly divided into three estates.
The Third Estate (about 97% of the population) paid most taxes while the
nobility and clergy were largely exempt. Enlightenment writers such as
Rousseau and Montesquieu spread ideas of liberty, equality, and popular
sovereignty. France had accumulated enormous debt, partly from the Seven Years'
War and from funding the American Revolution.

**Short-term causes.** Poor harvests in 1788 raised bread prices. King Louis XVI
called the Estates-General in May 1789 for the first time in 175 years. The
Third Estate broke away to form the National Assembly. On 14 July 1789 a Paris
crowd stormed the Bastille. The Declaration of the Rights of Man followed in
August 1789.
