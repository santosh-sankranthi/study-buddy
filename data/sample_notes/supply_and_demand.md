# Supply and Demand

The **law of demand** says that, all else equal, as the price of a good rises,
the quantity demanded falls. The **law of supply** says that as price rises, the
quantity supplied rises.

Plotting both gives the classic X-shaped diagram, with price on the vertical
axis and quantity on the horizontal. Where the curves cross is the
**equilibrium**: the price at which the amount buyers want equals the amount
sellers offer.

If price is above equilibrium there is a **surplus** and sellers cut prices. If
price is below equilibrium there is a **shortage** and buyers bid prices up.
A **shift** of a curve (caused by income, tastes, input costs, technology) is
different from a **movement along** a curve caused by a price change.
